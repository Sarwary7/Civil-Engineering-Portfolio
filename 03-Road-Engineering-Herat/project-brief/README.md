# Road Engineering Project Brief

This folder contains the original academic project brief and its English translation for the final phase of the road engineering project in Herat, Afghanistan.

The documents outline the design requirements, engineering assumptions, site constraints, and technical specifications provided for the academic project at Amirkabir University of Technology (AUT) – Tehran Polytechnic.

## Available Documents

| Document | Description | Language |
|---|---|---|
| [Road-Project-Brief.pdf](Road-Project-Brief.pdf) | English translation of the textual project requirements and technical specifications | English |
| [Phase2-Soorat-14012.pdf](Phase2-Soorat-14012.pdf) | Original academic project brief, including the site map, coordinate tables, and design requirements | Persian |

## Key Topics

- General project specifications and design assumptions
- Applicable road design standards (Iranian Publication No. 800-1)
- Site conditions and environmental constraints
- Borrow pits, disposal sites, and earthwork requirements
- Hydrology and surface drainage
- Traffic data and pavement design requirements
- Quantity takeoff and cost estimation
- Road signage and safety requirements

## Document Note

The English version is provided to make the academic project requirements accessible to international readers. The original Persian document is retained as the primary reference, including its maps and coordinate tables.

The project data and design requirements were provided for academic purposes and do not necessarily represent actual field conditions.
