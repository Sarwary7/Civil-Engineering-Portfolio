# Internship Reports

This folder contains the technical internship report documenting my three-month training experience in deep excavation, retaining structures, and construction site management at the Pourmousa Project.

## Internship Information

- **Type:** Professional Internship
- **Host Company:** Zamin Run Rosh Co.
- **Project:** Pourmousa Residential Building Project
- **Role:** Site Engineering Intern
- **Duration:** Three months
- **Report Date:** September 2025
- **Field:** Civil and Geotechnical Engineering

## Report Title

**Deep Excavation and Retaining Structures: Methods, Design Fundamentals, and Site Management**

## Technical Contents

The report is organized into five main chapters:

### Chapter 1 – Excavation

- Deep excavation and earthwork fundamentals
- Excavation machinery and construction methods
- Soil stabilization techniques
- Geotechnical investigations
- Excavation hazards and safety considerations

### Chapter 2 – Fundamentals of Retaining Structure Calculations

- Excavation stability and deformation
- Lateral earth pressure
- Retaining structure classifications
- Design principles and limit states
- Ground anchoring and anchor testing

### Chapter 3 – Retaining Structure Construction Methods

- Factors influencing excavation method selection
- Temporary retaining systems
- Integration of permanent building structures into excavation support systems

### Chapter 4 – Cross-Bracing (Struts)

- Internally braced excavation systems
- Steel struts and supporting structural members
- Steel mesh and shotcrete applications
- Cross-bracing design fundamentals
- Construction methods and connection considerations
- Pourmousa Project specifications
- Advantages and limitations of cross-bracing systems

### Chapter 5 – Construction Site and Excavation Management

- Excavation risk management
- Construction site safety
- Protection of adjacent buildings
- Site management responsibilities
- Excavation edge protection

## Practical and Academic Context

The report combines theoretical geotechnical and structural engineering concepts with observations and technical knowledge gained during the internship.

It documents the relationship between academic engineering principles and their practical application in a residential construction project.

The report is presented for academic documentation and professional portfolio purposes and does not constitute an independently prepared retaining structure design.
